IMT-Print lets you sign in with your mobile number, upload PDF documents, pay for extra print pages, find a nearby print kiosk, and collect the prints with a one-time SMS code. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

## Information we collect

### Account and sign-in

- Your 10-digit mobile number, used to send a one-time password (OTP) by SMS and to identify your account.
- The OTP is used only to sign you in. The print OTP is sent to your phone by SMS and is not shown in the app.
- On Android, the app may use the SMS User Consent API so you can approve filling the login code from that one message. The app does not read your other messages, and it does not request ongoing SMS access.
- A session token is stored on your device so you stay signed in. Signing out removes that token and the stored phone number from the device.

### Documents and print jobs

- PDF files you choose to upload, plus an optional document label, the selected kiosk, print options (including color or black-and-white), and page counts.
- Print-session details needed to create the job, apply any free-page allowance, and show print history (recent sessions tied to your account).

### Location

- If you use nearby kiosks, the app requests precise location (GPS) and sends your latitude and longitude to our servers only to return kiosks sorted by distance and to show them on a map.
- Location is not required to sign in. If you deny permission, nearby-kiosk search will not work.
- If you open directions, the app hands the kiosk coordinates to Google Maps (or another maps app you choose). That app's own policy then applies.

### Payments

- Extra pages are paid through Razorpay. We send Razorpay the amount, currency, order id, and your mobile number so the payment screen can be prefilled.
- Card, UPI, and bank details are collected by Razorpay and the payment app you choose (such as Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm). We do not store your full card or UPI PIN.
- We receive the payment result (order id, payment id, and a verification signature) so we can confirm the print job.

### On your device

- Language preference (English or Hindi).
- Session token and phone number, until you sign out or the session expires.
- A random request id is sent with API calls to help diagnose errors. It is not a permanent device identifier.

## How we use information

We use this information to:

- authenticate you and keep your session;
- upload documents and send print instructions to the kiosk you select;
- calculate free pages and charges;
- show nearby kiosks and print history;
- process payments for extra pages;
- secure the service, prevent abuse, and fix failures.

We do not sell your personal information. The app does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

## Who we share information with

- **Our servers**, at immortaltechnovation.com, which store your account, documents, and print jobs.
- **SMS providers**, which deliver login and print OTPs.
- **Razorpay** and the UPI or wallet app you select, for payments. See [Razorpay's privacy policy](https://razorpay.com/privacy/).
- **OpenStreetMap** tile servers, which receive map-tile requests (including approximate map location) when the kiosk map is shown. See the [OpenStreetMap privacy policy](https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy).
- **Google Maps**, only if you tap directions.
- **Authorities**, if required by law, or when needed to protect users, kiosks, or the service.

Kiosk operators receive the documents and job details required to print what you submitted.

## Retention

- The session token and phone number stay on the device until you sign out or the token expires.
- Account, print history, and uploaded PDFs are kept on our servers for as long as needed to complete printing, show history, meet tax and payment record rules, and resolve disputes, then deleted or de-identified.
- Print PDFs are deleted within [30] days after the job is completed or expires. Payment records are kept for [8] years where tax law requires it.

## Your choices

- You can deny location permission; nearby search will be unavailable.
- You can decline the SMS consent prompt and type the OTP yourself.
- You can sign out from the profile screen, which clears the session stored on the device.
- You can request access, correction, or deletion of your account, phone number, print history, and uploaded documents by emailing [privacy@yourdomain.com]. We may need to confirm that the request comes from the phone number on the account. We will respond within a reasonable time, and in any case as required by applicable law. Some payment records may be kept where the law requires it.

## Children

IMT-Print is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Security

Data is sent to our API over HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Keep your phone locked and do not share OTP messages.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The new effective date will appear at the top. Continued use of the app after the update means you accept the revised policy.
