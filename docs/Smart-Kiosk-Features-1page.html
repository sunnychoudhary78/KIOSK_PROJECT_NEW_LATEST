DESKTOP KIOSK

Smart Kiosk

Services in one touch

A public touchscreen for everyday tasks. Seven services sit on the home screen. Touch a tile to begin.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

01

Print

Scan a QR code and upload from any phone browser, with no app required. Or enter the OTP from the Smart Kiosk app, preview the pages, and collect them from the printer.

02

DigiLocker Print

Sign in to DigiLocker on the kiosk and choose the government documents you need. Preview each file, then print the ones you want.

03

Well Being

Check blood oxygen and heart rate with a short fingertip reading, or take a body temperature. Results appear on the screen in a few moments.

04

Phone Charging

Watch a short sponsored message, then plug in your phone. The screen follows the session until you finish and return to the menu.

05

Astrology

Open a birth chart in North or South Indian style, or photograph a palm for insights. You can also match two charts or see today’s panchang.

06

Fuel stations

Find petrol, diesel, CNG, and EV stations nearby, and filter by fuel type. Open a map, then scan a QR code to continue directions on your phone.

07

Local services

Look up help for AC, RO and washing machines, Two-wheeler mechanics, Carpenters, Electricians, Painters, Tailors, Scrap dealers, Gas-stove repair, Pigeon netting, and Puncture repair. Each listing shows the name, phone number, and location.

BUILT FOR PUBLIC USE

The same screen stays simple for every visitor who walks up to it.

Home

Tap Home when you are done. The next person starts with a clear screen.

Still there?

If the kiosk sits idle, a short countdown returns it to the menu.

At a glance

The home screen shows the time and local weather while you choose a service.

Out of paper

If the printer runs out of paper, printing pauses with a plain message.
